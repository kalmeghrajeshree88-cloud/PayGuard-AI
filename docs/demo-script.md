# 3-minute demo video script

Keep it under 3:00. Record the screen, speak clearly, no copyrighted music. Say once that payments run in PayPal Sandbox.

**0:00–0:20 Problem.** Fraud and scam payments are often approved because systems check the transaction but not whether it matches what the user says they are doing. PayGuard checks both, and only adds friction when the risk is high.

**0:20–0:50 Scenario 1.** Log in. Make payment → "1 · Normal". Analyze. Risk 0, approved. Pay with PayPal, approve in the sandbox, show the success message.

**0:50–1:30 Scenario 3.** "3 · New recipient". Show the risk gauge, the factor list, and the plain-English reason. Enter the one-time code, pay with PayPal, return to the app.

**1:30–2:10 Scenario 4.** "4 · Multiple anomalies". Show the scam-wording and device/location flags, risk 100, payment held. Point out that the AI extracted the intent but the rule engine made the decision.

**2:10–2:35 Dashboard.** Risk over time, decision counts, alerts, PayPal capture ID. Refund a completed payment.

**2:35–3:00 Close.** Explain the architecture in one sentence (AI extracts intent → rule-based risk engine → adaptive verification → PayPal), who it is for (wallets, marketplaces, small merchants), and that it learns: repeat scenario 3 and the recipient is now trusted.
